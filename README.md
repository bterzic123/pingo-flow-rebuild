# Growth Audit — Pingo AI

**Live:** https://bterzic123.github.io/pingo-flow-rebuild/

Five ideas to unlock more revenue from the traffic Pingo already has, each one rebuilt as a working screen in Pingo's own design. Prepared by Adapty from the live iOS flow, the App Store listing and pingo.ai.

| # | Idea | Potential outcome |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 01 | Offer one month when they close the paywall | ARPU +10–15% |
| 02 | Ask for an account after the paywall | CR +8% · ARPU +17% |
| 03 | Put their goal in the paywall headline | CR +15–20% |
| 04 | Show the price and your 4.8 on the first screen | CR +10–15% |
| 05 | Let the loader read their answers back | CR +10–15% |

The prototype opens on the overview. Each idea links to the screen that shows it, and the chips under the phone jump to any screen with answers already filled in. On a phone it runs full-screen.

Impact ranges are Adapty's expected effect from teardowns and A/B tests across subscription apps — not measured lift for Pingo AI. Every price, rating, quote and feature line comes from Pingo's own App Store listing, website and in-app screens.

python3 -m http.server 8000
